Tribis privacy policy

Version of 28 November 2026 (Tribis 1.21.0)

Tribis is an app for looking after your relationships with your people. It is made by an individual developer, Rodrigo Perucho Ferreiro (Spain), who publishes it under the name "Tribis". This policy explains what happens to your data in the Android app (Google Play) and in the web app (tribis-app.github.io).

In short

Everything you keep in Tribis stays on your device. There are no accounts, no cloud, no ads and no usage statistics inside the app.

Tribis has no servers and sends us nothing: we don't receive the data you keep, neither yours nor your people's. Something only leaves the device when you choose to open another app to write to someone (explained in "Writing to your people").

What Tribis keeps and where

What you type or choose: your name and the people you add (name, photo, birthday, phone numbers, email, Telegram and Instagram usernames, city, job, notes and other details), their connections and groups (also WhatsApp group links), the contacts you log, your plans and tasks, your Refuge sessions, your progress (energy, eras, treasures, streak) and your settings.

Everything is kept on your device: in the Android app, inside the app's private storage; in the web app, in your browser's storage for Tribis's address.

Tribis also keeps a few helper details on your device: an automatic copy of your data just before you import a backup (so it can be undone; deleted at the next import or when you open Tribis after 30 days), who you just wrote to (to ask you, when you come back, whether to log the contact; no longer used once you answer or after 12 hours) and, in the web app, the sounds that have already played, so they work offline.

Tribis keeps information about other people. Only keep what you're comfortable with, and look after their privacy as you would your own.

Phone contacts

Only when you tap "Choose contacts" does the Android app ask for permission to read your contacts. It reads names, birthdays, small photos and phone numbers, and shows them to you so you can choose. It never reads emails or addresses and never changes your contacts. It only keeps the people you tick and the details you decide to add to people you already have (for example "Add number").

In the web app you can choose contacts with the browser's picker (on Android it brings names, photos and phone numbers) or open a .vcf file (names, birthdays, photos and phone numbers). The file is read on your device and isn't kept; only what you choose is kept.

Writing to your people

When you tap "Write" and choose an app, Tribis opens it with that person's number, username or email and, if that app allows it, the message you prepared (Instagram doesn't get the message and "Call" only gets the number). "Another app…" opens the share menu with the message; on a computer without that menu, it copies the message for you to paste. Tribis doesn't send anything: you decide whether to send it. From then on, that app's own privacy rules apply.

On a computer, WhatsApp, Telegram and Instagram open in a browser tab, on their own websites, so the number or username (and the message) reach their servers when they open.

To show you only the apps you have, the Android app checks whether WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, Telegram and Instagram are installed (only those four) and whether the phone can make calls. That information stays on the phone.

Photos you share with Tribis

If you share a photo with Tribis from another app (for example someone's profile photo in WhatsApp), Tribis only uses it as the photo of the person you choose, and keeps it on your device. In the web app, the photo passes briefly through the browser's storage and is deleted from there as soon as Tribis opens it.

Backups

When you export, Tribis makes a file with your data (including phone numbers and, if you want, photos) and you decide where to save it or who to share it with (Downloads, Drive, WhatsApp…). What happens to that file is up to you and the services you use. You can protect it with a password, and we recommend it: without one, other apps with access to your files (for example to Downloads) can read it.

In the Android app, the last copy you exported also stays in the app's temporary folder until the next export.

The Android app asks Android not to include its data in cloud backups. Even so, when you move to a new phone with Android's own tool (by cable or phone to phone), Tribis's data may be copied to the new phone. To be safe, export now and then.

Internet connections

The Android app has no permission to use the internet: Android doesn't let it connect anywhere.

Google Play installs and updates the Android app and handles its own data under Google's privacy policy. As the developer, Google Play shows us general statistics (how many installs, from which countries and on which phone models), the crash reports Android collects and the reviews you post. None of that includes what you keep in Tribis.

The web app is downloaded from GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub (Microsoft). Your browser asks GitHub for files when you open the app, when it checks for a new version (when you open it and every hour while it's open) and the first time each sound plays. Like any website, GitHub receives technical details such as your IP address and browser type, under its own privacy statement. Those requests only bring the app's files: they carry nothing of what you keep in Tribis.

Apart from GitHub Pages (which serves the web app) and Google Play (which installs the Android one), Tribis uses no third-party services: no ads, statistics or crash reporting of its own. The fonts and drawings come inside the app, and on Android the sounds too.

What Tribis doesn't do

No accounts or sign-up. No ads. No statistics or tracking inside the app. We don't sell or share data. Tribis doesn't send notifications.

Deleting your data

On Android, uninstalling the app or clearing its data (phone Settings → Apps → Tribis → Storage) deletes everything.

In the web app, clear the site's data (tribis-app.github.io) in your browser settings. Uninstalling the web app doesn't always delete it.

Backups you exported aren't deleted by themselves: delete them where you saved them.

As we don't receive what you keep in Tribis, we have none of it to show, correct or delete: it's on your device, under your control.

Age

Tribis is meant for adults and isn't aimed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page and inside the app, with its date.

Contact

For any question, write to tribis.app@gmail.com. If you write to us, we receive your email address and what you tell us, and only use it to reply to you.
